Website privacy

Updated November 9, 2026

This page explains optional analytics on the Lucky 7 Towing website. You can browse the site and call us without allowing analytics.

Visits and phone-button clicks

When you allow analytics, Google Analytics receives website activity, including pages viewed, the page and button used to start a call, browser and device information, and the referring website. Google receives the network information needed to deliver its service. A phone-button click does not tell us whether a call connected, what was said, or whether a job was booked.

Our measurement code sends fixed page names and button locations. It removes URL queries and fragments and sends only the referring website’s origin. It does not send names, customer phone numbers, messages, or job details. Advertising features are disabled. The review and privacy pages are not measured.

Cookies and your choice

Analytics is off until you choose “Allow analytics.” Google’s analytics cookies identify a browser across visits; this site sets their lifetime to 30 days. Your analytics preference is stored in your browser until you change it or clear site data.

Use “Analytics choices” in the footer to change your preference. Choosing “Keep analytics off” stops this site’s analytics and removes its Google Analytics cookies. It does not erase data already sent to Google. We also keep analytics off when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, or when your preference cannot be saved.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Hosting and other links

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information to serve and protect the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Links to Google reviews, maps, and other websites open services with their own privacy practices.
